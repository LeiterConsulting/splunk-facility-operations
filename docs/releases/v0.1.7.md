# Facility Operations 0.1.7 — clearer operations stories and retained investigations

The app now opens with an **Operations story** that explains a business interruption, its supporting observations and the next audience decision. Sidebar icons and labels share a consistent left alignment. Detailed workspaces remain available inside native Splunk chrome.

## Experience

- One page-session case carries completed assistant findings, citations, exports and recorded demo decisions across workspaces. Navigating during an investigation keeps it running; explicit Stop or a new case cancels pending work. New presentation setup, guide restarts, Demo/Live changes and page refresh clear the case.
- The opening story presents a plain-language finding, business consequences, completion/response/aging measures, competing diagnostics and contextual investigation actions. Stale or unknown observations cannot supply current performance facts.
- **Start three-minute overview** follows five stages. **Start guided walkthrough** follows all eight stages, including optional explicit assistant investigation and partial recovery. **Explore freely** and **Play continuous loop** remain available. Skipped stage observations are never invented in the export.
- The desktop guide has a reserved reading column, concise source evidence and a **What we learned** explanation. It highlights relevant evidence without covering it. Long-response arrows stay inside the app evidence area below native Splunk chrome. Compact layouts retain the pinned guide.
- Audience decisions prioritize relevant returned evidence and name a useful deliverable: an incident handoff, technical investigation, executive situation brief, containment review, assurance package, change decision or site/continuity handoff.
- Completed responses include a four-part summary computed from the frozen returned snapshot: Finding, Supporting evidence, Uncertainty and Next check. The provider-generated narrative remains separate and inspectable. Factual scope receipts and technical details expand on demand. Provider history requests retain the existing last-six-message bound.
- **Download customer brief** creates a readable Markdown document with current measures, the latest frozen investigation and capture scope, local decisions, uncertainties and customer proof-of-concept requirements. The story JSON also retains the latest completed investigation. Neither export claims measured operational savings or final business completion.
- Settings offers clinical service interruption, manufacturing quality release and retail checkout/fulfillment starting stories. Audience/industry/use-case selection remains in Settings. Presentation readiness reports returned coverage and saved provider selection; connection testing remains an explicit Settings action.
- **How Splunk supports this** explains the observation-to-decision path and distinguishes working capabilities, simulated actions and customer integrations still needed.

See the [presentation guide](https://github.com/LeiterConsulting/splunk-facility-operations/blob/v0.1.7/docs/demo-guide.md) for the short overview, full walkthrough, retained investigations and customer brief.

## Installation

App ID **splunk_facility_operations**, version **0.1.7**, build **15**. Download **splunk_facility_operations-0.1.7.tar.gz** from the release assets and upload it compressed through **Apps → Manage Apps → Install app from file**. The `.spl` installer contains identical bytes. Git, Node and npm are not required for installation.

For upgrades, back up local overrides, credentials and populated live inventory outside the release checkout. Select **Upgrade app**, follow deployment/restart prompts and reopen the app. The release inventory is header-only; provider credentials remain in Splunk credential storage. Follow the [installation guide](https://github.com/LeiterConsulting/splunk-facility-operations/blob/v0.1.7/docs/getting-started.md#download-and-install-from-github).
